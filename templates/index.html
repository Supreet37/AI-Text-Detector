Questions

Things people ask.

How accurate is the detection?

Our current model ensemble reaches 90.5% accuracy on mixed-domain benchmarks. Accuracy is highest on text longer than 100 words.

Do you store the text I submit?

No. Submissions are processed in memory and discarded immediately after analysis.

Which AI models can it detect?

Veritext is trained on outputs from major LLM families (GPT, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA) and updated periodically.

Can I use it on code?

Yes. The classifier detects source code and marks it separately instead of forcing an AI/Human verdict.
